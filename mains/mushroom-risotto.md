# Mushroom Risotto Recipe

## Ingredients
- 200g risotto rice
- 250g mushrooms, sliced
- 750ml hot vegetable stock
- olive oil
- 1 small onion, finely chopped
- 1 garlic clove, finely chopped
- 50ml white wine (optional)
- 25g butter
- 40g grated Parmesan
- salt, pepper and parsley

## Instructions
- heat the olive oil in a saucepan and soften the onion
- add the mushrooms and cook until browned, then add the garlic
- add the rice and stir for 1–2 minutes
- add the wine, if using, and let it evaporate
- add the hot stock a ladle at a time, stirring frequently and letting each addition absorb before adding more
- cook for around 18–20 minutes, until the rice is tender with a slight bite
- remove from the heat, stir in the butter and Parmesan, season and sprinkle with parsley
